Common questions

Frequently asked questions

I'm a nervous patient. Can you help?

Absolutely. We set aside longer first appointments for nervous patients, agree a stop-signal before we begin, and offer sedation options for more involved treatments. Note your anxiety in the booking form — we'll make sure the right clinician calls you first.

How much does treatment cost?

Every treatment fee is published on this page — no "call for a quote". Check-ups start from £58. Sample 0% finance is available over 6–12 months for treatments over £350.

How quickly can I get an emergency appointment?

Emergency slots are released at 8:30am daily. Select "Emergency care" in the booking form — these are flagged as priority. For genuine emergencies outside hours, call 111 for NHS urgent dental advice.

What happens after I submit my appointment request?

You receive an instant on-screen confirmation with a booking reference (NGD-XXXXX) and an email confirmation. The practice confirms your slot within 2 hours during opening hours and sends an SMS reminder 24h before.

Do you offer NHS treatment?

Sample content — a live build reflects the client's NHS/private status. Many Didsbury practices are mixed or private list. The booking form will confirm your options.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the "Track your booking" panel with your NGD reference, or call us on 0161 XXX XXXX. We ask for 48 hours' notice where possible to free the slot for another patient.

Is 0% finance really available?

Sample content — in a live build, finance is provided via a regulated third-party lender. 0% APR available on treatments over £350, over 6–24 months, subject to status and credit check.

Is my personal data safe?

Your data is stored only in our secure database and used only to process your appointment. We collect the minimum personal data needed. See our Privacy Notice for full details of your UK GDPR rights.
